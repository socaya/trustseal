Honest Assurance — What we don’t over-claim

A platform-sealed PDF proves the TRUSTSEAL key signed those exact bytes. It does not automatically prove every named signatory produced an individual PKI key signature, nor that a document is legally binding under every jurisdiction. A drawn signature is visual evidence of appearance, not standalone PKI proof — and we state that transparently on every verification verdict. Read the full Trust & Security notes →
