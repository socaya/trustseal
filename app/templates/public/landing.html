How it works

  1. 1

    Prepare

    Upload a PDF, add participants, pick sequential or parallel routing and set reminders.

  2. 2

    Sign, stamp & seal

    Signers review via a token-scoped page. Authorised custodians apply organizational stamps. The final PDF is sealed with a platform PAdES signature backed by AWS KMS and anchored into the chained audit log.

  3. 3

    Verify independently

    Anyone can upload a sealed PDF plus its evidence digest and receive a verdict that separates document integrity, certificate trust, revocation state — and checks that were not possible.

What we don’t claim

A platform-sealed PDF proves the TRUSTSEAL key signed these bytes. It does not automatically prove every named signatory produced an individual cryptographic signature, nor that the document is legally binding under any specific jurisdiction. A drawn signature is visual evidence of appearance, not PKI proof. We say so on every result page. Read the full trust & security notes.
