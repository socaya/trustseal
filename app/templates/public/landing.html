What you can do

Prepare signing envelopes

Upload a PDF, add participants, pick sequential or parallel routing, set reminders and send. Every action records an immutable event.

Collect reviews & decisions

Signers review through a token-scoped page. Acknowledgements are bound to a specific document digest; declines are first-class outcomes, not errors.

Apply organizational stamps

Design, approve, assign custody and apply stamps on authorized documents. Each application links to the evidence record.

Seal the final PDF

A platform PAdES seal is applied through an AWS KMS-backed key with a trusted timestamp and captured revocation status. The sealed bytes are stored, hashed and anchored into a chained audit log.

Verify evidence publicly

Anyone can upload a sealed PDF plus its evidence digest and receive a clearly classified verdict — document integrity, certificate trust, revocation state and any checks we could not complete.

Export the audit trail

Chained-hash SecurityEvents, signed anchors and validation dossiers are retained per policy and exportable for auditors.

What TRUSTSEAL does not claim

Honest assurance scope

A platform-signed PDF demonstrates that a TRUSTSEAL certificate sealed the bytes with a configured key — it does not automatically prove that every displayed human signatory separately produced a cryptographic signature, nor that the document is legally binding under any specific jurisdiction. A drawn signature appearance is evidence of appearance under a documented process, not a PKI-grade individual signature. A visual organizational stamp is a workflow record, not a cryptographic seal. Where a check could not run, we say so explicitly.
